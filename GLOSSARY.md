# Sticker Studio

Sticker Studio creates and edits Telegram sticker packs from photos and videos.

## Language

**Pack**: A user's collection of stickers, either a draft or a published Telegram
sticker pack. An imported pack is an independent copy of another pack.

**Source**: A photo or video prepared for editing, including an editable copy of
an existing sticker. Sources are temporary and may expire.

**Render**: A sticker produced from a source and its edits, ready for publication.

**Preview**: A browser-compatible representation used to view a source or sticker.
It does not change the sticker published in Telegram.

**Publication**: Adding a render to a pack or replacing a specific sticker with it.
Repeating a completed publication does not add or replace another sticker.

**Studio**: The workspace for preparing a source, editing it into a render,
previewing the result and publishing it to a pack.
